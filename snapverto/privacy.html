SnapVerto

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains how devtrollo handles information in SnapVerto, an Android app that reads and translates text from your screen or camera. You can use SnapVerto without creating an account.

1. Screen captures, camera images, and text

When you start a screen capture and approve Android’s screen-sharing prompt, SnapVerto captures an image and lets you select the area to read. When you use the camera feature, SnapVerto uses the camera preview and the photo you choose to take to read text.

SnapVerto uses Google ML Kit to recognize text, detect its language, and translate it on your device. Screen captures, photos, recognized text, and translations stay on your device during this processing. SnapVerto does not send this content to the developer, Google’s servers, or the advertising SDK.

If you tap Copy, SnapVerto puts the translation on the Android clipboard so you can paste it into another app.

2. Android permissions

  • Screen capture. Android asks you to authorize screen sharing for the capture.
  • Camera. Android camera permission lets SnapVerto show the camera preview and take a photo for text recognition.
  • Display over other apps. This permission lets SnapVerto show its floating buttons and translation overlays. You can turn off the buttons in SnapVerto or revoke overlay access in Android settings.
  • Internet and network access. Google services use the network to download models, transmit SDK diagnostics or usage metrics, and support advertising and ad consent.

3. Google ML Kit

SnapVerto may download a translation model from Google when a language needs it. Google ML Kit may also contact Google for updates or device compatibility information and send usage and performance metrics. Google uses those metrics to maintain and improve ML Kit, diagnose problems, and detect abuse.

Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy

4. Advertising and privacy choices

SnapVerto may display ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your settings and the ad request, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google may use the IP address to estimate your general location. Google encrypts this SDK data in transit.

Where required, SnapVerto uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to show an advertising consent form. When Privacy settings is available in SnapVerto, you can open it to review or change your advertising choices. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

5. Storage and deletion

SnapVerto handles captures and translation results temporarily while providing the feature. It does not create a capture or translation history or keep copies on developer servers. Text you copy to the clipboard is managed by Android and can remain available through your keyboard’s clipboard history, depending on your settings.

The app stores preferences on your device, and downloaded language models may remain there for reuse. You can remove stored app data and downloaded translation models by clearing SnapVerto’s app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Google handles retention and deletion of data it receives through its services under its privacy policy.

6. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, contact devtrollo at devtrollo@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and keep the correspondence as needed to handle your request.
