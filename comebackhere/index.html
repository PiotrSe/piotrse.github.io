FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few quick answers before you head out. The app is intentionally simple, just like the idea behind it.

Is ComeBackHERE free?

Yes. The app is free to download and contains ads. There is no subscription mentioned on its Google Play listing.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app is designed to work without an account, and saved locations stay on your device.

Does it replace a navigation app?

No. ComeBackHERE focuses on saving a point and showing the direction and distance back to it. Use your preferred navigation app for turn-by-turn road directions.

What can I save?

Any location you may want to return to: a parking spot, campsite, hotel, meeting point, trailhead or landmark.
